web-fastq-human-remover Split reads against a reference genome, entirely in your browser

Split reads against a reference

Separate the reads that belong to a reference genome from those that do not — host, vector, PhiX, ribosomal RNA, any FASTA. Files never leave your machine.

100% local — nothing is uploaded. All computation happens in this tab; there is no server. FASTQ files are streamed and never loaded into memory in full, whatever their size.
Unofficial, independent port. The approach — a Cuckoo filter over canonical spaced seeds — is taken from Cleanifier (MIT licence), with no connection to its authors and without their endorsement. The index is rebuilt locally from your own FASTA rather than loaded from the published human index. For publishable work, run Cleanifier natively.

Spaced seeds tolerate substitutions better than contiguous k-mers of the same weight: on synthetic reads at 10% divergence, the Cleanifier seed recovered 14.6 points more contaminated reads than a contiguous k=29. They cost roughly 3× in throughput.

2 · Samples

Drop your FASTQ files or click to browse
.fastq[.gz] / .fq[.gz] — _1/_2 and _R1/_R2 pairs are grouped automatically

For a pair, the worse mate decides both reads — splitting one without its mate would break mate pairing downstream.

Both sides are written. Each sample yields *.matched.fastq (reads found in the reference) and *.unmatched.fastq (the rest). Which one you keep is your call — the tool does not assume that matching the reference means contamination.
